Turn the work you already do into a verified, always-current case for growth — for IBM iX designers & their managers.

The problem: A designer's real impact is scattered across Slack, email, MyScore, YourLearning & Thanks@IBM. Growth IQ auto-unifies it into one live picture, aligned to the iX design dimensions — so growth is evidence-based, not a once-a-year scramble.

👤 For Employees

Your live growth story

  • ▹Auto-captured contributions — verified work pulled from MyScore, YourLearning & peer praise in Slack/Outlook. No manual logging.
  • ▹One growth picture — quarter contributions, transparent Impact Score & Band-readiness %.
  • ▹Recognition Inbox — review, edit & send detected shout-outs for sign-off.
  • ▹AI Career Coach — grounded in your profile; names your biggest gap & the next move.
  • ▹Readiness, not entitlement — shows distance to the band bar; decision stays with the manager.

👥 For Managers

Team Intelligence Hub

  • ▹Whole-team view — contributions, avg Impact Score & who's promotion-ready across reports.
  • ▹Verify & approve — sign off contributions with evidence attached; adjust impact level.
  • ▹Fairness signals — flags overdue 1:1s & members whose pace has slipped.
  • ▹Review & prep kits — generate performance-review material from real, verified data.
  • ▹Recognition prompts — nudges to recognize great work as it happens.
The payoff: Less busywork, a fair & current case for growth — and managers coaching from evidence, all speaking one iX language.
